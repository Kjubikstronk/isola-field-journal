# Isola Field Journal

**A free live companion for Plant Tycoon on Windows.** It sits on top of your
game, reads your nursery as you play, and tells you which pots to cross, what to
plant next, and when no cross can get you there.

- **Website:** https://kjubikstronk.github.io/isola-field-journal/
- **Download:** [IsolaFieldJournal-setup.exe](https://github.com/Kjubikstronk/isola-field-journal/releases/latest/download/IsolaFieldJournal-setup.exe) (1.3 MB)
- **Breeding calculator** (no install needed): https://kjubikstronk.github.io/isola-field-journal/calculator.html

You need your own copy of [Plant Tycoon on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/16120/Plant_Tycoon/).

## Install

1. Download and run the installer. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC"
   because the app is not code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Open Isola Field Journal and click **Launch Plant Tycoon**.
3. Play. **Ctrl+E** opens or folds the panel.

## Is it safe?

- It only reads. It never writes to the game or to your saves.
- It never goes online: no account, no ads, no tracking, no update checks.
- It contains nothing from the game; it reads the game's pictures from your own
  installation.
