From your plants

Add the plants you have, growing or as seed, then pick the one you want. You get the fewest crosses that make it, or the reason no cross can.

    The six Magic Plants

    Finding all six is the goal of the game. None is tier 4, so each can be bred from ordinary stock. Each card gives one cross that makes it; "Route from my plants" works out the way from what you have.

    Magic Tree of Fragrance

    Fourpetal Maple

    T1 Its effect: more visitors and rarer insects.

    Needs the basic soil and water.

    One cross that makes it: Blazing Bamboo with Aureus Rare Oak.

    Rose of Joy

    Rosaceae Fern

    T2 Its effect: a steady crowd of visitors.

    Needs the basic soil and water, with attention.

    One cross that makes it: Mystic Gladiatus with Viola Ananas.

    Golden Berries of Wealth

    Aureus Scandens

    T3 Its effect: customers pay more, for everything.

    Needs Bio-organic Soil ($850) and Filtered Water ($650).

    One cross that makes it: Fourpetal Reptans with Venomous Grass.

    Fabled Rose of Isola

    Fabled Lemonbush

    T3 Its effect: sells for the most of any plant.

    Needs Bio-organic Soil ($850) and Filtered Water ($650).

    One cross that makes it: Jalapa Fanleaf with Fragrant Bamboo.

    Fruit of Life

    Mela Rare Oak

    T3 Its effect: plants resist infestation and flower more.

    Needs Bio-organic Soil ($850) and Filtered Water ($650).

    One cross that makes it: Venomous Maple with Citrus Pipe Cactus.

    Flower of Knowledge

    Nox Orchid

    T3 Its effect: every plant priced near its true value.

    Needs Bio-organic Soil ($850) and Filtered Water ($650).

    One cross that makes it: Daisy Grass with Bluestar Astera.

    All 529 species

    Leaves down the side, flowers along the top, both from lowest to highest. The colour is the plant's tier: the higher it is, the better the soil and water it needs. Magic Plants have a gold ring. Pick a square to see the plant.
